2 What HashShelf is

HashShelf turns a reading list into a link. There are no accounts, no login, and no profile. Your shelf lives in the link itself: the characters after the # in a HashShelf URL are your books, compressed and checksummed — not a lookup code for a record about you.

Two kinds of links exist, and the distinction matters:

  • Full ("long") links carry the entire shelf in the URL fragment. Because browsers never transmit the fragment to a server, the content of a long link is never received, seen, or stored by us. It exists only on the devices of people who hold the link.
  • Short links (hashshelf.com/s/…) are created only when you choose to shorten a shelf. When you do, a copy of that shelf ("snapshot") is stored on our server and re-served at that address with a preview. Short links are the only user content we store.

Book titles, authors, covers, and subjects are supplied by third-party sources (see Section 10). The Service is provided free of charge and is supported by affiliate links (Section 9).
